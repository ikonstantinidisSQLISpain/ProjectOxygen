# Introduction

The Oxygen project is composed of multiple data that can be classiffied in different ways, however, for this project it will be classified based on their sources. This sources are:

* APP
* Whoz
* External
* Internal

Both APP and Whoz are applications that the company uses, "Internal" represents data generated by the company from sources different to these apps, while "External" represents data that comes from sources external to the company (e.g. LinkedIn).

## APP

APP is an internal company application that is used primarly by the users to log the time spent on different projects and request for holidays or leaves. Based on the provided data, the following database entities can be identified:

* **Workers:** Self-explanatory, represents the employees.
* **Project:** Originally named as "activity" and self-explanatory. It represents the individual project that is being done by the company.
* **Timesheet:** Montly time spent by each worker on each project/activity.
* **Leaves:** Represent the leave requests made by the workers.
* **Worklogs:** If `timesheet` represented the montly logs, this data represents the individual daily logs. [[#^note-1|Note 1]]

## Internal

Internal is the name used to categorize all the data related to the company's organization, services and communication with clients. Based on the provided data, the following database entities can be identified:

* **Unit (Business and Cost Units):** A unit is a subdivision of the company that act as an independent company, with the purpose of the main company.
* **Society:** A society represents a set of units of different kind.
* **Entity:** An entity represents a set of units.
* **Department:** A department is a group of workers that have a set of characteristics.
* **Site:** It is a location.
* **Zone:** It is an area. [[^note-3|Note 3]]
* **Service Line:** A service line is a set of products or services offered to clients.

## Whoz

Whoz is defined as *"a platform designed for IT, consulting and engineering organizaztions to centralize talent management."* It allows users to expose their digital CVs and it allows the recruiters to manage talents. Based on the provided data, the following database entities can be identified:

* **Users:** It represents the individual user from Whoz platform.
* **Profiles:** If the user wants to expose a digital CV it may create a profile.
    * **Concept:** It appears as a FK, we don't know what this is. It only appears in "aptitudes", and aptitudes seem to be skills.
    * **Position:** It is the job position that was added to a profile.
* **Skills:** A skill represent the individual known ability that a user can add to a profile. [[#^note-4|Note 4]]
* **Certifications:** A certification is a document that verifies a fact. This entity represents the individual certification a user may add to its profiles.
* **Qualifications:** Based on the data it is impossible to know what this is, only the ID is provided.
    * **Attatchment:** The attatchment of a *certification*.
* **Talents:** Talent is the object used to store the data that recruiters need about the potential future workers during both the recruitment phase and the phase as employee.
    * **Photo:** The photo of a *talent*.
* **Workspaces:** At this moment, there is nothing that can be done to know what does this represent. Only the ID is provided. It has been guess it is the associated Whoz space for an `entity`.
* **Federations:** Same as *workspace*. It is guess that it is the Whoz tenant associated to sqli.


## Relationships

* A `unit` belongs to a `society` and a `society` has multiple `units`.
* A `unit` belongs to an `entity` and a `entity` has multiple `units`.
* A `worker` works at a specific `unit` and a `unit` may have multiple `workers`.
* A `worker` works at a specific `site` and multiple may work on the same `site`.
* A `worker` may have one `tl_rh` (worker, talent lead or human resource), and it may manager multiple `workers`.
* A `worker` may have one `direct_manager` (worker), and it may manage multiple `workers`.
* A `department` may work at specific `service_line` at specific `zone` (Triple relation).
* A `project` may belong to multiple `service_line` and viceversa.
* A `project` may belong to multiple `department` and viceversa.
* **Collaboration status:** Represent the collaboration between each worker and each service line, under each department over time. [[#^note-2|Note 2]] and [[#^note-2-1|Note 2.1]].
* **worklogs** and **timesheet** represent the relationg between each worker and each project over time.
* A `user` might be either be `created` or `modified` by another `user`.
* A `user` may belong to one or multiple `workspace` and a `workspace` may have multiple `users`.
* A `user` may belong to one or multiple `federation` and a `federation` may have multiple `users`.
* A `profile` might be either be `created` or `modified` by a `user`.
* A `profile` belongs to a `federation` and a `federation` may have multiple `profiles`. [[#^note-5 | Note 5]]
* A `profile` may have multiple `skills` and a `skill` is present in one or multiple `profiles`.
    * A `position` may have multiple `skills` and a `skill` might be present in one or mltiple `positions`.
* A `profile` may have multiple `qualifications` and a `qualification` is present in one or multiple `profiles`.
    * A `position` may have multiple `qualifications` and a `qualification` might be present in multiple `positions`.
* A `profile` may have multiple `certification` and a `certification` is present in one or multiple `profiles`.
* A `talent` might be either be `created`, `modified` or have `last_confirmation_date_modified` by a `user`.
* A `talent` might be `user` and a `user` might be a `talent`.
* A `talent` may have multiple `qualifications` and a `qualification` is present in one or multiple `talents`.
* A `talent` belongs to a `workspace` and a `workspace` may have multiple `talents`.
* A `talent` may have a `manager` (talent) and a `manager` may manage multiple `talents`.
* A `talent` may have a `mentor` (talent) and a `mentor` may mentor multiple `talents`.
* A `talent` may have an `externalId` and an `externalId` may appear in multiple `talents`.
* A `talent` has a `photo` and one only, and the `photo` appears only in one `talent`.
* A `skill` belongs to a `federation` and a `federation` may have multiple `skills`.
* A `skill` may have a `parent skill` and it may `parent multiple skills`.
* A `skill` may be part of multiple `skills` and viceversa.
* A `skill` may have in different languages the next attributes: `name`, `description`, `terms`, `hiddent_terms`, `wikipedia_link`, `depiction`.
* A `certification` belongs to a `profile` and a `profile` may have multiple `certifications`.
* A `certification` belongs to a `talent` and a `talent` may have multiple `certifications`. [[#^note-6 | Note 6]]
* A `certification` belongs to a `workspace` and a `workspace` may have multiple `certifications`.
* A `certification` might be `created` or `modified` by a `user`.
* A `certification` may have multiple `qualifications` and a `qualification` may appear in multiple `certifications`.
* A `certification` may have only one `attatchment` and an `attatchment` only belongs to a `certification`.
* A `certification` may have multiple `skills` and a `skill` may be present in multiple `certifications`.
